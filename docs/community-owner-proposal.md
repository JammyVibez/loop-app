# Community Owner Proposal — bring your community to Loop

This is a pitch you can send to community owners (Discord admins, Telegram group
leads, newsletter writers, club organizers) to get them to try Loop. Two versions
are included:

1. **The short DM** — copy/paste into a chat message.
2. **The long pitch** — for a partnership email or a meetup announcement.

Before sending to a lot of people, make sure the app is deployed and stable, and
that a couple of real communities already exist on the platform — "join us, it's
new" converts far worse than "a few communities already hang out here."

---

## 1. The short DM (copy/paste)

> Hey {name} 👋
>
> I'm building Loop — a social app where communities live *inside* the feed
> instead of being an afterthought. Every community gets its own Circle: a home
> page, a shared post feed, events, and DMs that actually work.
>
> I set up a Circle for your community already ({circle_link}) — you'd be the
> owner, fully in control of roles, posts, and events. No setup, no cost, no
> commitment. If it's not for you, no hard feelings.
>
> A few things your members would get:
> - **A real community feed** — posts, polls, duels, reels, and code sharing
>   (yes, actual code blocks with syntax highlighting) all in one place.
> - **Events that work** — create an event, members RSVP, everyone gets a
>   reminder. No more "who's coming?" threads.
> - **Co-Loop** — two members co-author one post together in realtime, from a
>   DM. Nobody else has this; it's a great "look what this app can do" moment.
> - **Push notifications** — members actually see when something happens in the
>   Circle, not just when they happen to open the app.
>
> Want to see it? {app_link} — I can walk you through it in 5 minutes whenever
> you're free.

## 2. The long pitch (email / announcement)

**Subject: A home for your community that actually feels like a community**

Community owners put real work into their Discord servers, Telegram groups, and
mailing lists — and then watch engagement live and die by whichever app people
happen to have open. Loop is a social platform built the other way: the
community *is* the product.

**What your community gets**

- **Circles.** Your community gets a permanent home — a profile page, a shared
  feed, member roles, and moderation controls. It's a Discord server, an event
  calendar, and a feed in one place, without the setup.
- **Events with RSVPs.** Create an event in a couple of taps. Members RSVP,
  see who's coming, and get pushed a reminder so they actually show up.
- **A feed that's yours.** Posts, polls, duels, reels, and even code snippets
  with syntax highlighting. Community members can post and branch each other's
  work — a built-in "remix culture" that keeps a community creating.
- **Co-Loop.** Members co-author a single post together in realtime, right from
  a DM. It's the kind of feature that makes people screenshot the app and send
  it to friends — which is exactly how communities grow.
- **Monetization-ready.** Gifting, coins, boost, and sponsor cosmetics give
  active communities real ways to reward contributors.

**Why now**

Loop is in active, fast-moving development — the features above ship weekly.
That means your community gets to shape the product (your feedback lands), and
early communities get featured placement as the platform grows. First mover
advantage, no cost, no lock-in.

**What I'm asking**

Ten minutes. Create your Circle, post one announcement, and invite a handful of
members to test the event + RSVP flow. If it's not a fit, you lose nothing.

Reply to this email and I'll set up your Circle for you and walk you through it.

## 3. What to say to specific community types

- **Discord/Telegram admins:** "It's a companion space — a feed + events for
  your existing server. Members already love Discord; this gives you a home for
  posts that scroll away too fast."
- **Club / event organizers:** "Free event pages with RSVPs and reminders.
  No more spreadsheets."
- **Developer communities:** "Real code blocks, forking, and co-authoring.
  Your members can show off projects instead of pasting screenshots."
- **Creator communities:** "Gifting, coins, boosts, and sponsor cosmetics —
  your fans can actually support you inside the app."

---

## 4. Checklist before you send it

- [ ] App is deployed and the landing page is polished
- [ ] At least one real community is already active on Loop
- [ ] Your own Circle exists and looks good (avatar, banner, description)
- [ ] A sample event is live so they can see the RSVP flow
- [ ] Push notifications work on a test device
- [ ] The signup flow works in an incognito window (Google + email)

If anything on that list is broken, fix it before messaging anyone — a broken
first impression is worse than not messaging at all.
